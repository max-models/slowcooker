## What it does

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Any geometry" icon="puzzle">
		Flux-surface averages of a GVEC equilibrium, tokamak or stellarator, go into TORAX through its CHEASE format, in memory, without changing TORAX.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Neutral beams" icon="rocket">
		freeofcharge deposits the beams and slows the fast ions down; the heating, fuelling and current follow the plasma from one segment of the run to the next.
	</Card>
	<Card title="The half dimension" icon="random">
		GVEC is re-solved with TORAX's pressure, and the geometry factors follow.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Stellarator transport" icon="setting">
		A neoclassical model for the 1/ν and √ν regimes with the ambipolar radial electric field, as a TORAX plug-in (prefactors still to be verified).
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
